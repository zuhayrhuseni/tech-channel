description: >
  The one command the creator invokes to make a whole episode end-to-end.
  Apply when the user says "make a video/episode", "new episode about X",
  "build episode NNN", or hands you a topic and expects a finished render.
  Drives the full pipeline as an explicit checklist — research → beats →
  script → grade → cues → acquire → animate → grade → anonymity — stopping
  only at the human gates it is not allowed to cross (topic approval, beat
  approval, recording, publish).
---

You are producing a complete episode of a faceless educational tech channel
for early-career and aspiring engineers. This skill is the spine; it calls
the other skills and the two grader agents at the right moments. Read
`CLAUDE.md`, `GOALS.md`, `docs/research/PLAYBOOK.md`, and
`docs/research/asset-sourcing.md` before you touch anything — the hard rules
there (anonymity, public sources, `[VERIFY]`, source-everything) override
everything below.

Work autonomously through each step. **Stop only at the four human gates**
marked STOP — topic approval, beat approval, the recording session, and
publish. Everything between gates is yours to do without asking.

## Where things live (real paths — use these exactly)

```
episodes/NNN-slug/
  script.yaml          hand-authored: narration + inline visual cues (step 3)
  narration.txt        generated by make_narration.py (step 5)
  narration.wav        HUMAN recording, or narration.draft.wav for animatic
  narration.wav.json   whisper-cli word output (step 8)
  timing.json          generated by make_timing.py — mark→frame ground truth
  assets.json          generated by npm run acquire (step 7)
  out/final.mp4        the render (step 8)
src/eNNN/EpisodeNNN.tsx   the composition you write (step 8)
src/components/theme.ts   design tokens — import, never hardcode colors
capture/README.md         the visual-cue schema for step 6
```

Zero-pad and slug the folder: `episodes/004-tls-handshake/`. Only
`script.yaml` and the recording are authored by hand; everything else is
generated — if a generated file looks wrong, fix its source and re-run,
never hand-edit it.

## Step 1 · Research + validate the topic  → STOP for approval

Skills: **`faceless-playbook`** (does this topic fit the channel, the series,
the cadence?) then **`deep-research`** for anything non-trivial. If the user
handed you a topic, still validate every load-bearing claim.

- Every factual claim gets a real URL you fetched, not memory. Public
  sources only (BLS, official docs, company engineering blogs, primary
  reporting). No employer knowledge, no insider examples (CLAUDE.md rules
  1–2).
- Anything you cannot verify against a public source becomes a
  `[VERIFY: claim]` flag carried into the script — never assert it.
- Confirm it fits the channel: an engineering concept, industry analysis,
  or breaking-into-big-tech angle aimed at early-career engineers.

**STOP.** Present the topic, the angle, and the source list (URLs). Get an
explicit go before drafting. Do not pick the topic for the creator unless
they told you to.

---

## Step 2 · Draft beats  → STOP for approval

Propose the beat outline only — ids, one line each, the hook, the argument
spine (but/therefore, not and-then), the re-hooks, the ending. Package
first (PLAYBOOK #10): draft a title + thumbnail direction now; the
thumbnail must not repeat the title.

Hook discipline (PLAYBOOK #3): stakes → target → transformation, never
answer the core question in the hook, first visual in the first seconds.

**STOP.** Get the beats approved before writing a single narration line.
Rewriting a graded script is expensive; a wrong outline is cheap.

---

## Step 3 · Write `script.yaml`

Skill: **`script-voice`** (mandatory — this is the voice taste pass).

Write into `episodes/NNN-slug/script.yaml`, matching the structure of
`episodes/002-two-job-markets/script.yaml`: top-level `title`, `id`,
`target_length`, `lane`, `type`, a `# SOURCES` comment block with every URL,
then `beats:` — each beat has `id`, `narration` (YAML folded `>`), `visual:`,
and `notes:` for the source citation.

Voice rules (from the skill, non-negotiable):
- Conversational, telling-a-friend, warm and slow. Contractions,
  second person, but/therefore causation, ellipses for warm slowdown.
- Kill AI tells: at most one staccato fragment triad, at most one
  "it's not X it's Y", no "here's the thing"/"that's the problem"
  signposts, no "-ing" significance trailers.
- Rhythm: vary length — per 10 sentences ≥1 under 6 words, ≥1 over 25,
  under 70% in the 12–22 band.
- Personality: 1–2 opinions stated as yours, 1–2 self-corrections, ≤1
  digression with a marked exit, 4–8 deadpan jokes per 10 min, one
  callback in the last third.
- House cues inline: `/` (~0.35s) `//` (~0.9s) `[beat] [breath]`
  `[dry][up][down][smile]` `**punch**` (one per sentence) and
  `<mark id="x"/>` on the exact word a visual must sync to.

Beats that are deliberate silence use `visual: none` (GOALS.md — those
render background only; do not fill them).

---

## Step 4 · Script-grader gate  (MANDATORY)

Run the **`script-grader`** agent on the episode's `script.yaml`.

It returns a **PASS or REVISE** verdict with a score table and line-level
defects. **A REVISE verdict gets rewritten** (loop back to step 3 with the
`script-voice` skill, address the named defects) and re-graded. Do not
proceed to recording or timing on a REVISE. Do not argue with the grader —
fix the script.

---

## Step 5 · Reading copy + word/VERIFY check

```bash
python3 make_narration.py episodes/NNN-slug/script.yaml
```

Writes `narration.txt` (teleprompter copy — never edit it by hand). It
prints the word count, estimated runtime, and flags any remaining
`[VERIFY]`. Rules:

- Any surviving `[VERIFY]` must be resolved (verify → assert, or cut the
  claim) back in `script.yaml`, then re-run this step.
- Under ~1,200 words means the video is too short for a mid-roll — pad the
  outline in `script.yaml`, not `narration.txt`, and re-run.

## Step 6 · Place visual cues

Skill: **`visual-direction`** — decides *what* appears per beat (fetched
image vs screenshot vs rebuilt chart vs FakeTerminal vs keyword card),
writes the acquire cues, and places `<mark id=/>` sync points. Schema
reference: **`capture/README.md`**. Pair with **`motion-density`** for how a
chosen scene moves. Every capturable beat's `visual:` gets a cue that
`acquire` can resolve. Supported cue kinds:

```yaml
visual:
  kind: image                      # or `meme` — license-clean stock via query
  query: "confused person at laptop"
  annotate: { circle: [960, 400, 120] }   # optional cx,cy,r in 1920x1080
  land_at: some_mark               # sync to a <mark id="..."/>

visual:
  kind: browser_capture            # screenshot a real page/headline as a receipt
  source_url: "https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TLS"
  selector: "article header"       # optional crop
  annotate: { circle: [1200, 260, 90] }
  land_at: some_mark
```

- `screen_recording` is phase-2 — the browser launcher supports it but the
  scroll script + `OffthreadVideo` scene aren't wired yet; don't spec it.
- Charts and data-viz are rebuilt in code from raw public data (PLAYBOOK
  #8: BLS is public domain; FRED third-party series are not) — those are a
  code beat in step 8, not an `acquire` cue.
- Diagrams, code, and data live full-bleed on the background; cards are
  containers for annotations only (PLAYBOOK variety rules). Rotate the
  entrance grammar — ≥4 kinds, never the same pattern >3 elements in a row.
- Every `source_url` and every image `query` must trace to a public source
  from step 1. Nothing decorative — every element is narrated within ~1s.

## Step 7 · Acquire assets  (through the gates)

```bash
npm run acquire episodes/NNN-slug
```

Reads `script.yaml`, resolves every capturable cue, and writes
`episodes/NNN-slug/assets.json` + `public/ep-<slug>/`, appending
`credits.md`. It routes every capture through:

- the **anonymity gate** (`capture/lib/anonymity.ts`): OCR + DOM text
  scanned for names and `/Users/<name>/` · `/home/<name>/` paths — a hit
  discards the capture and fails the run;
- the **license gate** (`capture/lib/license.ts`): BY-SA / NC / ND / GFDL /
  unknown rejected, each kept asset credited in `credits.md`.

A non-zero exit names the failing cue. **Never work around a gate failure**
— fix the cue (different source/query) and re-run. The render never runs on
a missing or leaked asset. (`forbidden.json` holds the real identity
strings and is gitignored; make sure the creator's name is in it.)

---

## Step 8 · Animate + render  (autonomous — GOALS.md is the contract)

This is the "Build video NNN. Read GOALS.md." step. Skills:
**`motion-density`** (density/pacing/broadcast-package rules the video-grader
enforces), **`remotion-best-practices`** / **`remotion-render`** for the
Remotion mechanics. Sequence (GOALS.md):

1. **Align.** Run `whisper-cli` on the recording with word-level output to
   produce `narration.wav.json`
   (`whisper-cli ... -ml 1 -oj -of episodes/NNN-slug/narration.wav`), then:
   ```bash
   python3 make_timing.py episodes/NNN-slug/
   ```
   which writes `timing.json` (captions + per-beat/per-mark frames). Every
   `<mark>` must resolve to a spoken word — if one can't, **stop and name
   it** (GOALS.md stop-and-ask #1); it means the script was edited after
   recording. Don't approximate.
2. **Build.** Write `src/eNNN/EpisodeNNN.tsx` — one `<Sequence>` per beat,
   driven off `timing.json` frames. Import design tokens from
   `src/components/theme.ts` (`theme.bg/ink/dim/accent/warm/up/down`,
   `REVEAL_FRAMES`) — never hardcode colors. Reuse
   `src/components/{AnnotatedImage,LayerPeel,TopicIcons,DivergingLines,`
   `NarrowingEntry,AmbientBackground}`; read `index.ts` before making a new
   one. Feed each `assets.json` entry into `<AnnotatedImage>`. Motion floor:
   entrances 200–400ms ease-out (spring damping ~200), visuals land on or
   ~3 frames before their mark, keywords held ≥24 frames then docked to a
   label, hold end states ≥1s, screen never static >3s (motion-density).
3. **Register.** Add the `<Composition>` to `src/Root.tsx` (1920×1080,
   fps 30, `durationInFrames` from the exported constant). Include
   `narration.wav` as an `<Audio>` in the composition so the mux happens at
   render. **If integration is handled downstream, do not edit `Root.tsx`,
   `src/components/index.ts`, or `package.json` — instead return the exact
   export + `<Composition>` registration lines needed.**
4. **Render.** `npx remotion render EpisodeNNN out/final.mp4` (h264 crf
   16–18, AAC 320k, 1080p30). Fix your own render errors, missing deps, and
   Remotion API problems until `out/final.mp4` exists and plays — that's the
   GOALS.md definition of done.

Voice/TTS note: `make_tts.py` (skill: `elevenlabs-narration`) generates a
**draft** `narration.draft.wav` + exact `timing.json` from the ElevenLabs
clone, so the animatic can be built and the script iterated *before* a take
is recorded. It is **draft-only**. Every published episode uses the
**human** `narration.wav` — ElevenLabs output carries SynthID + C2PA markers,
a permanent strike-signal on a faceless channel (voice-clone-vs-recording.md).
Re-recording invalidates `timing.json`; regenerate it, never reuse.

The human recording itself (how-to steps 3–5: read `narration.txt`, record
`raw.m4a`, cut snaps in Audacity, `ffmpeg loudnorm` → `narration.wav`) is a
**STOP** gate — you cannot record for the creator. Build the animatic on the
draft; swap to `narration.wav` and re-time when they deliver it.

---

## Step 9 · Video-grader gate  (MANDATORY)

Run the **`video-grader`** agent on the episode folder. It grades
`out/final.mp4` against `docs/animation-review-rubric.md` — sync, motion
craft, readability, pacing, technical delivery, hook — and returns
**PASS or REVISE** with exact frames to fix.

**A render is not done until it PASSes.** On REVISE, fix the named defects
in the composition (or the script/cues if that's the root cause) and
re-render, then re-grade. This gate precedes and never replaces the human
review.

---

## Step 10 · Final anonymity sweep  → STOP for human review

Before handing back:
- Confirm `credits.md` exists and every asset is attributed.
- Sanity-check every frame with rendered text for file paths, usernames,
  employer names, credentials (the video-grader also does this, but verify).
- Confirm no `[VERIFY]` survived into the render and every claim still
  traces to a step-1 URL.

**STOP.** The final full-screen anonymity read and the publish decision are
human steps (how-to steps 7–8). **Never publish, upload, or push** — a
leaked frame is permanent (GOALS.md). Hand back `out/final.mp4`, the source
list, and `credits.md`, and let the human take it from there.

---

## Gate summary

| Gate | Type | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Topic + sources | STOP (human) | approve before drafting |
| Beats + packaging | STOP (human) | approve before writing script |
| `script-grader` | agent | REVISE → rewrite w/ `script-voice`, re-grade |
| Recording | STOP (human) | human records `narration.wav`; draft TTS meanwhile |
| `video-grader` | agent | REVISE → fix + re-render until PASS |
| Anonymity + publish | STOP (human) | never publish/upload/push |

Autonomous between gates. Source everything with a URL. Fix the source, not
the generated file.
